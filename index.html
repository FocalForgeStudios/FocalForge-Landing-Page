Independent by nature. Ugandan by origin. Built by Andrew to bring visual craft and digital thinking into one studio.

Founded by Andrew in Kampala, Focal Forge is built on a simple belief: African ideas deserve world-class execution. We bring together visual storytelling, design and engineering to make work that feels considered, culturally grounded and impossible to mistake for anyone else.
